# Product Overview

Civic Social Platform is a full-stack civic issue reporting and workflow management system that connects citizens with government departments and community volunteers to address local infrastructure and service issues.

## Core Functionality

Citizens report issues (potholes, streetlights, waste management, etc.) with location, photos, and severity. The platform routes issues through a multi-stage workflow involving:

- **Citizens**: Report issues, vote on priorities, verify resolutions
- **Volunteers**: Claim and resolve community-fixable issues
- **Officers**: Review, assign, and manage departmental work
- **Workers**: Execute assigned tasks from officers
- **Admins**: Oversee users, departments, analytics, and system-wide operations

## Key Features

- Geospatial issue tracking with heatmaps and proximity search
- Priority-based routing and escalation system
- Department-based assignment with coverage areas
- Dual resolution paths: volunteer community fixes or official department work
- Real-time notifications and status updates
- Analytics dashboard with trend analysis
- Role-based access control and approval workflows
